GyanMirai JLPT Test App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy covers the GyanMirai JLPT Test mobile app for Android and iOS (the "App"), published by GyanMirai ("we," "our," or "us"). It explains what data the App uses, who else receives it, and the choices you have. For the gyanmirai.com website, see our website Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • The App works fully without an account. Signing in is optional.
  • Where the App offers sign-in, you sign in with your GyanMirai account: the same account works on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts.
  • If you don't sign in, your test results and answers, mistakes review, saved questions and settings are stored only on your device and we do not receive them.
  • If you sign in, we store your account (email address and basic profile details) and a copy of your test results, mistakes review, saved questions, answered-question counts and study settings on our servers, so they can be backed up and synced across your devices.
  • You can delete your account and its synced data in the App (You → Delete account) or at any time by email. Deleting your GyanMirai account deletes it everywhere: on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. No ad is ever shown while a test is running.
  • The optional one-time ad-free purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • Reminders and the question of the day are notifications scheduled on your phone; they are not sent from our servers.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

The tests, questions and explanations are built into the App. Your own data is kept in local storage on your device: the tests you finished (which test, its questions, your answers, the questions you flagged, the time you took and your score), tests you have started but not finished, your mistakes review (a review schedule for each question you missed or skipped), your saved questions, how many questions you answer (counted per 15-minute period, for your daily goal), the days you opened the App and your study settings. If you don't sign in, this data is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

If you allow notifications, the App schedules the question of the day and a practice reminder on your device; no push server is involved and nothing is sent to us. The App does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

3. Your account (optional)

Where the App offers sign-in, you can sign in to back up your test history and progress and sync them between devices. No feature of the App requires an account.

One GyanMirai account

Signing in uses your GyanMirai account: the same account works on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts. If you don't have one yet, signing in creates it. You sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email address, whichever the App offers.

What we receive and store

  • Account details: if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the App; with an email code, we receive only your email address. Our backend stores your account as a user record containing your email address, the sign-in method, a random user ID we assign and, if you use Google, a Google account identifier and the basic profile details Google provides.
  • gyanmirai.com profile: because the account is your GyanMirai account, it also has a gyanmirai.com profile, created automatically the first time you sign in, with your email address, your name and profile picture link (if Google provides them) and the sign-in method. What you do on gyanmirai.com is covered by our website Privacy Policy.
  • Test history and progress: the tests you finished (which test, its questions, your answers, flagged questions, the time you took and your score), your mistakes review schedule and the questions you removed from it, your saved questions and when you saved them, how many questions you answered per 15-minute period, and your study settings. This is linked to your user ID.
  • Not uploaded: tests you have not finished yet, the days you opened the App, your notification settings, the question of the day and other preferences for this device stay on your phone.

How we use it

Only to provide the account: to sign you in, back up your test history and progress and keep them in sync across your devices, to let you use the same account on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps, and to let your ad-free purchase follow your account. We do not use account or progress data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with Supabase, our backend provider, in its Tokyo (Japan) region, and are only accessible to your account. If you live outside Japan, this means your data is transferred to and processed in Japan. Supabase processes it on our behalf under its privacy policy.

Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out stops syncing; the data on your phone stays. To delete your account, open You → Delete account in the App. This immediately deletes your GyanMirai account everywhere (on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app), together with everything synced to it, including your gyanmirai.com profile, website progress and test results. The data on your phone is kept until you uninstall the App.

While GyanMirai Pro bought on gyanmirai.com (a subscription or an exam-season pass, not the App's ad-free purchase) is still running on your account, the App can't delete the account: cancel the subscription on gyanmirai.com and try again once your Pro has ended, or email hello@gyanmirai.com and we will delete the account for you. You can ask us to delete your account by email at any time; see how to delete your account.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not gone ad-free, the App shows banner ads on the Home and Tests screens and a full-screen ad after a finished practice test (never after your first finished test, and never after a mock test). No ad is ever shown while a test is running. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system
  • ad impressions, clicks, diagnostic data and crash logs

Google uses this data to show, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from You → Ad privacy choices in the App.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission before accessing your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Going ad-free stops all ad requests from the App.

5. Purchases

Going ad-free is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card or bank details.

To confirm and restore your purchase, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives the store purchase receipt, an app user ID and basic device information (model, OS version, app version, country). If you are not signed in, the app user ID is an anonymous ID generated on your device; if you are signed in, it is your account's user ID, so your purchase follows your account. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Links to our website

Some buttons in the App (for example Same test on gyanmirai.com) open gyanmirai.com in your browser. These links carry campaign tags (such as utm_source=test_app) so we can see that the visit came from the App; they contain no identifier for you. What happens on the website is covered by our website Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The App is intended for teenagers and adults (13+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security and retention

Data sent by the App travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and synced data can only be read by the account it belongs to. On-device data stays until you uninstall the App.

If you sign in, your account and synced data are kept until you delete your account, including finished tests the App no longer lists on your phone. When you delete it, it is removed from our servers straight away, and from any backups within 30 days. Data held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. If you have not signed in, we do not hold your test data on our servers, and most requests can be handled by the ad and purchase choices above or by uninstalling the App. If you have an account, you can delete it and its synced data: use You → Delete account in the App or follow the steps on our account deletion page. For access, correction or anything else, email us from your account's email address and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes

If we change how the App handles your data, we will update this policy before the new version is released and change the effective date above.
